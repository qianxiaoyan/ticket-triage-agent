# Billing and Invoices

## Payment failure and dunning

When a recurring payment fails, we retry on day 1, day 3, and day 7. The workspace stays active during this window. On day 8 the workspace downgrades to read-only until payment succeeds. Customers on the Enterprise tier keep access while a finance contact is resolving the issue, up to 30 days.

## Upgrading mid-cycle

Upgrades take effect immediately. We prorate the remaining days of the current cycle and charge the difference on the next invoice. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current cycle; no partial refund is issued for unused time after a downgrade.

## Annual plans and seat changes

Annual plans are billed upfront for a fixed seat count. Adding seats mid-term is prorated. Removing seats mid-term does not generate a credit; the seats remain available until renewal.

## Duplicate or incorrect charges

If a customer believes they were charged twice, ask for the invoice number and the payment date before escalating. Most duplicate charges are pending authorizations that drop off within 5 business days. A true duplicate is refunded within 3 business days after finance confirmation.

## Tax and VAT

Prices exclude tax. EU business customers with a valid VAT ID are charged under the reverse-charge mechanism and must self-assess VAT. We cannot issue a retroactive VAT ID change for invoices older than 90 days.

## Updating payment method

Only workspace owners and billing admins can change the payment method. Changes apply to the next invoice; the current invoice must still be paid with the original method.
