# Support SLA and Priority Definitions

## Priority definitions

P1 means the customer cannot use the product at all, or there is a confirmed security incident, data loss, or a full production outage. P2 means a major feature is broken with no reasonable workaround. P3 means a question, a request, a cosmetic issue, or a bug with an acceptable workaround.

## Response targets by tier

Free tier: P1 within 24 hours, P2 within 3 business days, P3 best effort. Pro tier: P1 within 8 business hours, P2 within 2 business days, P3 within 4 business days. Enterprise: P1 within 1 hour with on-call paging, P2 within 4 business hours, P3 within 2 business days.

## Escalation rules

Escalate to a human immediately when: the ticket reports a security incident or suspected account takeover; the customer is Enterprise and the issue blocks production; the customer explicitly requests a human; the request involves a legal threat, a chargeback, or a data deletion confirmation; or the assistant cannot ground an answer in the knowledge base.

## Things support must not do

Support must not promise a refund outside the refund policy, must not disclose internal infrastructure details, must not confirm roadmap dates, and must not reset 2FA for a user who cannot pass identity verification.

## Out-of-scope requests

Feature requests and bug reports go to the product tracker, not to a support resolution. Sales, procurement, and security questionnaire requests route to the account team.
