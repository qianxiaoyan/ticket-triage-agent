# Refund Policy

## Trial period

New workspaces get a 14-day trial. A refund is available in full if the customer cancels within 14 days of the first paid charge and has consumed less than 20 percent of their plan quota. Refunds after 14 days are prorated only for annual plans with more than 6 months remaining.

## Monthly plans

Monthly subscriptions are non-refundable once charged, except when the charge was caused by our error. As a goodwill gesture, support may issue a one-time service credit of up to one month for accounts with no previous credits.

## Annual plans

Annual plans can be refunded prorated within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, refunds are only issued for documented service outages that breached the SLA.

## SLA breach credits

When uptime falls below the committed level in a calendar month, affected workspaces are eligible for service credits: 10 percent of monthly fee for uptime below 99.9 percent, 25 percent for below 99.5 percent. Credits must be requested within 30 days of the incident and are applied to a future invoice, not paid out in cash.

## Chargebacks

A chargeback immediately suspends the workspace. We dispute chargebacks only when there is a signed order form or a usage record showing active use after the disputed charge. Reinstatement requires the chargeback to be withdrawn or repaid.

## Non-refundable items

Professional services hours, onboarding packages, and custom integration work are non-refundable once delivered.
